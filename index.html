What's under the hood

Everything a multi-stop trip needs, in one flow

No spreadsheets, no ten open tabs. Add a city, it asks what you're doing there — then it prices it, dates it, and puts it on the calendar for you.

Stops

Ordered itinerary

Add cities in the order you'll visit them, set arrival and departure dates, reorder anytime.

Activities

Built into each stop

Attach sightseeing, food, and adventure picks to a city — cost and duration come along with it.

Budget

Priced automatically

Stay, transport, activities, and meals roll up into a live total, with an alert if you're over budget.
